What the third frame exposes
Urgency reads nowhere

An artisan arriving in ten minutes and a job three weeks out are the same row, same weight, same 48pt icon. Only an eleven-pixel chip separates them.

Nothing is actionable in place

"Awaiting your confirmation" is a thing the customer must do, and the only affordance is a chevron into the detail screen. Same for tracking a live job.

The long chip breaks the row

"Awaiting your confirmation" is 26 characters in an inline pill. It either wraps to three lines or squeezes the service name — the vocabulary and the layout disagree.

A generation behind Home

System font, #F97316, gray-100 hairlines, 22pt title. Home and onboarding now run Instrument Sans on sand with #E4620A. Put them side by side and they are two apps.

Empty is the common case

At launch most opens land on frame one. A receipt icon and a sentence is honest but inert — there is no route from here back to booking something.

Counts sit on the filters

Three chips, three counts, and the customer usually wants one of them. Worth asking whether Active/Completed/Cancelled is the split, or whether time is.
