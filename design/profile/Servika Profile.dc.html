What's worth deciding
Eight tiles, eight hues

Blue, pink, green, red, pink again, orange, slate, teal. The colour carries no meaning — it is decoration on a system that otherwise runs one orange, and it is the loudest thing on the tab.

Two things appear twice

Notifications and Settings are in the header AND in the grid. Bookings and Messages are in the grid AND in the tab bar underneath. Four of the eight tiles are second routes to somewhere already one tap away.

The stats are inert

1 / 0 / 0 on a real account. They take a fifth of the first screen, none of them is tappable, and Bookings already counts the same things on its own filters.

The support card outshouts everything

A three-stop gradient, a 48pt headset and a white button — for a mailto: link that duplicates the Help & Support tile directly above it.

Identity is three greys deep

Name, phone and email stack at 17 / 13 / 13 in gray-900, gray-500 and gray-400, and the email truncates anyway. Worth asking which of the three the customer actually needs to see here.

A generation behind, with the seam showing

System font, #F97316, gray-100 hairlines, 24pt radii, a gradient. The tab bar drawn beneath it is already v2 — Instrument Sans, #E4620A, one hairline, no gradient. The two are touching.
